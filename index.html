Methodology & limitations

Data: User-selected CSV and GeoJSON sources.
Business selection: Detected name and coordinate fields.
Proximity: Approximate distance calculated from business coordinates.
Routing: Public OSRM service; estimated routes are not guaranteed travel times.
Facilities: OpenStreetMap data; completeness varies.
Privacy: Do not publish confidential household coordinates, restricted EA samples or personal records to public GitHub.
