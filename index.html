Methodology & limitations

Data: source files selected by the user or configured public repository.
Spatial filtering: point-in-map and administrative attribute filtering where fields exist.
Proximity: approximate geodesic radius around a chosen map location.
Routing: public OSRM driving route, dependent on OpenStreetMap road coverage.
Facilities: OpenStreetMap/Overpass features; completeness and positional accuracy vary.
Population: interpret counts according to the source's definitions, dates, geography, and methodology.
Privacy: do not upload confidential household coordinates, restricted EA samples, or personal records to public GitHub.
